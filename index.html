About Samtech Nexus Limited

Bridging Digital Solutions, Essential Services & Modern Tech

Founded by Samuel Kathuri, Samtech Nexus Limited is a client-focused digital agency and tech hub built to simplify your personal, academic, and business needs. Located in Nairobi at Meru House, Room 108, we serve walk-in clients in the city as well as customers across Kenya through seamless online support.

Whether you are launching a brand, applying for vital travel and government documents, or upgrading your daily gadgets, we deliver reliable results under one roof. We understand that tasks like KRA iTax compliance, passport bookings, and visa applications require accuracy and strict confidentiality—while businesses and individuals need standout graphic design, modern websites, and genuine electronics they can depend on.

Why Choose Samtech Nexus Limited?

  • Walk-In & Online Convenience: Visit us in person at Meru House, Room 108 (Nairobi) or get served remotely from anywhere in Kenya.
  • Countrywide Delivery: Order smartphones, laptops, and accessories with fast, secure delivery straight to your doorstep anywhere in the country.
  • Strict Confidentiality & Accuracy: Your personal details, KRA credentials, and travel documents are handled with the highest level of privacy and precision.
  • Fast Turnaround: From urgent flyers and CVs to online applications, we work promptly so you never miss a deadline.
